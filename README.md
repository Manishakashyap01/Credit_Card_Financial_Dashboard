Credit Card Financial Dashboard

An end-to-end Power BI project that analyzes a year of credit card transactions (2023) and turns them into weekly, customer and transaction dashboards.

Objective

To simulate a real-world dashboard scenario and practice generating actionable insights using Power BI by analyzing credit card transactions from 2023, focusing on weekly trends, customer behavior, and financial performance.


Key Insights

- Overall revenue is 57M: interest 8M and transaction amount 46M
- Male customers contribute 31M and female customers 26M
- Blue and Silver cards contribute 93% of overall revenue, with Blue alone at 47.2M
- TX, NY and CA together contribute 68% of revenue
- Businessman is the top revenue job (17.7M), followed by White-collar (10.3M)
- Q4 is the highest revenue quarter (14.5M)
- Swipe is the top payment method (36M), while Online is only 4M
- Overall activation rate is 57.5%
- Overall delinquent rate is 6.06%
- Week 53 (31st Dec): revenue increased 28.8% week over week (933,134 to 1,201,601)

Customers
- Male: 31M, Female: 26M
- Businessman: 17.7M, White-collar: 10.3M

Geography
- TX, NY, CA: 68% of revenue

Behavior
- Swipe: 36M, Online: 4M
- Activation rate: 57.5%
- Delinquent rate: 6.06%
- Week 53: +28.8% revenue WoW

 Recommendations

1. Upsell Gold and Platinum cards to high-limit, low-utilization Blue customers (to be validated with credit limit and utilization data)
2. Run activation campaigns to lift the 57.5% activation rate
3. Promote online spending, which is only 4M against 36M from swipe
4. Focus acquisition on the Businessman and White-collar segments
5. Track the 6.06% delinquent rate by card category and utilization band

 Tools

- Power BI (data modeling, DAX measures, dashboards)
- Excel / CSV (data inspection)
- PowerPoint (project presentation)
